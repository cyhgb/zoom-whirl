r(φ) — the zoom-whirl signature

Flat, level stretches are whirl phases — the particle loops near the unstable circular radius. Sharp excursions are the zoom out to (and back from) apoapsis.

Integrated in Schwarzschild coordinates, equatorial plane, units G = c = M = 1, from d²u/dφ² = 1/L² − u + 3u² where u = 1/r — RK4, fixed step. The cubic 3u² term is the general-relativistic correction absent from the Newtonian orbit equation; it is what makes an unstable circular orbit — and zoom-whirl behavior — possible at all.
